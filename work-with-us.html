Why Work With Us?

We're a co-op. That means instead of just collecting a paycheck, you directly share in the profits of everything we build together. 50% of net profits are shared among contributors based on their work, while the founder covers all upfront costs for servers, tools, and AI.

What We Do

  • Commercial game server hosting for classic and modern titles
  • Game testing, customer support, and mod development
  • Code & website updates (Linux and Windows administration)
  • Game development in Unity and Unreal
  • Business applications for real-world clients

Why It's Worth It

  • It's genuinely fun to build games with a passionate team
  • Hands-on Linux & Windows sysadmin experience
  • Resume-ready projects in hosting, coding, and game development
  • Opportunities to learn Unity, Unreal, PHP, AI tools, and more
  • 50% of profits shared with contributors
  • Use our tools for your own side projects — we'll even help where we can

Who Should Apply?

Gamers, coders, modders, and developers who want real-world experience and the thrill of building something together. If you've dreamed of making games, running live servers, or launching your own ideas with professional tools, this is your chance.
